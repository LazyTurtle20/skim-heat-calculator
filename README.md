# Skim Heat Scoring System Documentation

## 1. Scoring System Overview  
The skim heat scoring system is designed to evaluate performance in skimboarding contests, ensuring that judges have a consistent framework to score heats fairly and accurately. The scoring is based on a combination of various inputs, weighted to reflect their importance in the overall performance.

## 2. Core Judging Inputs  
- **Technical Difficulty**: The complexity of maneuvers performed.  
- **Execution Quality**: How well the maneuvers are executed.  
- **Amateur to Pro Rating**: Classifying the level of skill of the participant.  

## 3. Criterion Weighting  
Each input is assigned a weight that contributes to the overall score. For example:  
- Technical Difficulty: 40%  
- Execution Quality: 50%  
- Amateur to Pro Rating: 10%  

## 4. Heat Base Score  
The base score for each heat is calculated as follows:  
- **Heat Base Score = (Technical Difficulty * 0.4) + (Execution Quality * 0.5) + (Amateur to Pro Rating * 0.1)**

## 5. Modifiers and Bonuses  
Additional modifiers can be added for special achievements during the heat, such as:  
- **High-Performance Bonus**: +2 points for completing a series of advanced maneuvers.  
- **Consistency Bonus**: +1 point for completing all maneuvers without falls.

## 6. Failure Penalties  
Points may be deducted for failure to complete a maneuver or any falls that occur during the performance.  
- **Fall Penalty**: -3 points per fall.  

## 7. Offset Caps  
To maintain fair scoring, offset caps are applied to prevent scores from exceeding certain limits.
- **Maximum Heat Score**: 90 points.  

## 8. Final Heat Score Formula  
The final heat score is calculated using the formula:
- **Final Heat Score = Heat Base Score + Modifiers - Failure Penalties**

## 9. Tandem Division Math  
In tandem divisions, scores are combined from both participants:
- **Combined Score = Participant 1 Score + Participant 2 Score**

## 10. Calculator Usage Instructions  
To use the skim heat calculator, enter the following inputs into the respective fields:
- Technical Difficulty  
- Execution Quality  
- Rating (Amateur/Pro)  

The calculator will output the heat base score, final score, and any applicable modifiers or penalties.

**Mathematical Examples**:  
1. If a participant scores:  
   - Technical Difficulty: 7  
   - Execution Quality: 8  
   - Rating: 9  
   Then the scores would compute as:  
   - Heat Base Score = (7 * 0.4) + (8 * 0.5) + (9 * 0.1) = 7.8  
   - Adding a High-Performance Bonus of +2 results in 9.8. 

2. If a participant falls during the performance, the calculation with a fall penalty would be:
   - Final Heat Score = 9.8 - 3 = 6.8  

Please ensure to follow all instructions regarding calculating scores accurately and consistently in every heat.
